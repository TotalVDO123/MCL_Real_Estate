Created for Agents, by Agents

Multiple Clients List is a software designed for agents by agents. This software allows users to search through active and inactive buyer agreements, giving all agents a deeper understanding of the prospecting buyers. MCL was created to solve the problems that exist in the current market, like limited access to client information, time-consuming paperwork, and lack of communication.

Consistency is the key to success!

The majority of the agents who have persisted in the industry for an extended period will tell you that consistency is key to their success. Consistency begins with a firm and forthright connection with their clients. Setting realistic expectations and obtaining a signed Buyer's Agreement are the first steps. These critical components distinguish them from the competition and help them rise above the competitive real estate market.
